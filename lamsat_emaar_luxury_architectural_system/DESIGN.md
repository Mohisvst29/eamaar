## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, editorial identity tailored specifically for high-end turnkey renovation, heritage restoration, and luxury interior fit-outs in Saudi Arabia’s Eastern Province. 

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Grounded Luxury:** Rooted in architectural gravitas, tactile materials (natural travertine, Riyadh limestone, smoked oak, brushed bronze), and deliberate restraint.
- **Architectural Quietude:** Clean, unapologetic whitespace. Avoid frivolous tech trends, rounded pill buttons, high-intensity blurs, or neon accents. Every component should feel like cut stone, machined brass, or heavy linen stationery.
- **Bilingual Authority (RTL-First):** Rooted deeply in nuanced modern Arabic typography while offering seamless, equally deliberate Latin typographic pairings for international clientele and supplier documents.

### Design Movement
**Architectural Minimalism with Material Realism.** Characterized by monolithic grid layouts, hair-thin divider rules reminiscent of architectural drafting, generous negative space, bone-and-charcoal tonal contrasts, and tactile hover states mimicking the subtle shift of natural light over brushed stone.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural stone, limestone quarries, aged patinas, and deep artisanal charcoal wood finishes.

### Core Palette
- **Canvas Base (`#F9F8F6`):** Warm alabaster/linen, soft on the eyes, avoiding digital clinical whites.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F3EFEA`):** Light parchment and pale travertine tone for nested sections and visual rhythm.
- **Primary Ink (`#181615`):** Deep espresso carbon black; conveys mass, confidence, and editorial precision.
- **Secondary Ink / Body Text (`#2B2623`):** Warm smoked charcoal for long-form Arabic readability.
- **Muted Taupe (`#68625B`):** Architectural annotation, specifications, metadata, and secondary captions.
- **Brushed Architectural Bronze (`#C09B66`):** Primary accent used exclusively for selective emphasis, key milestones, active focal indicators, and bespoke calls-to-action.
- **Aged Desert Gold (`#9E7B48`):** Deep bronze shade for hover interactions and high-contrast borders against bright backgrounds.
- **Stone Border (`#E6E0D6`):** Ultra-subtle, hair-thin contour that anchors components like structural joints.

## Typography

The typographic system relies on **IBM Plex Sans** (with fallback to IBM Plex Sans Arabic). It offers crisp structural balance, geometric discipline, and exceptional balance across Arabic right-to-left scripts and English engineering specifications.

### Editorial Execution
- **Headings:** Delivered with deliberate line heights to accommodate Arabic diacritics and ascenders gracefully. Never compressed or tight.
- **Labels & Project Codes:** Set in `label-caps` with intentional positive letter-spacing for Latin engineering labels (e.g., project IDs, square meter dimensions, material specs). For Arabic labels, preserve standard cursive flow while retaining medium-to-bold weights.
- **Hierarchy:** Rely on size contrast and muted tone gradations (Charcoal to Taupe) rather than excessive font-weight changes.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an architectural drafting grid inspired by classical proportions and monumental spatial planning.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, `margin-desktop: 4rem`, `gutter-desktop: 2.5rem`. Wide editorial margins frame project photography and material boards.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid structure with tight outer framing (`margin: 1.25rem`) maximizing image scale and portfolio inspection.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Vertical rhythm must feel open and measured. Large sectional divisions leverage `space-xl` (and multiples thereof) to isolate case studies like curated gallery walls.
- Avoid cluttered clusters. Information density is kept low, ensuring every material sample, blueprint, and specification has room to breathe.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eliminates conventional digital drop shadows and blur effects in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Hairline Joinery**.

### Depth Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Foundation Canvas):** `#F9F8F6` provides the warm baseline plane.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Trays):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Crisp Alabaster (`#F5F2EC`), defined not by drop shadows but by a crisp, continuous 1px border (`#E6E0D6`).
- **Tier 2 (Floating Inspections / Dialogues):** Used for full-screen blueprint previews and high-res finish showcases. Framed with a 1px `#2B2623` outline and accompanied by an ultra-diffused, warm ambient undertone (`rgba(24, 22, 21, 0.04)` with a 32px blur), resembling physical museum glass framing.
- **Tactile Transitions:** On hover, cards do not elevate along a Z-axis. Instead, their border transitions smoothly from limestone taupe (`#E6E0D6`) to brushed bronze (`#C09B66`), mirroring an architect’s guiding hand.

## Shapes

The architectural tone mandates absolute precision: **Sharp geometric profiles (`roundedness: 0`)**.

- Every card, modal, button, and image frame uses clean, 90-degree squared corners.
- This sharp geometry echoes stone masonry, precision joinery, cut marble slabs, and physical project plans.
- Circular or heavily radiused shapes are prohibited, except for true geometric circular badges or status dots when strictly required.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#181615` background, `#F9F8F6` typography, squared edges (`0px` radius). Generous horizontal padding (`space-lg`), medium weight. Hover state reveals an inner hairline border in brushed bronze (`#C09B66`) with a subtle fade to `#2B2623`.
- **Secondary / Architectural Outline:** Transparent surface, 1px solid `#181615` border. On hover, fills with `#F3EFEA`.
- **Tertiary Accent:** Minimalist text link accompanied by a sharp bronze directional arrow indicator (pointing Left for Arabic RTL, Right for Latin).

### Cards & Project Showcases
- White `#FFFFFF` or pale linen `#F5F2EC` background with a mandatory 1px `#E6E0D6` perimeter border.
- Photography spans edge-to-edge across the top of the card with zero border-radius.
- Typography within the card is strictly structured: Category/Area in `label-caps` (Taupe), Project Title in `headline-sm` (Espresso), and Location/Scope details in `body-sm`.

### Input Fields & Booking Requests
- Understated architectural inputs: Background `#FFFFFF`, bordered on all sides by 1px `#E6E0D6` (or alternatively an editorial bottom-line rule only).
- Focus state: Border transitions crisp and unblurred to `#181615` or `#C09B66`.
- Labels float permanently above in `label-caps` using `#68625B`.

### Material Swatches & Chips
- Crisp rectangular chips (`0px` radius) with 1px borders. Unselected chips sit on `#F3EFEA` with `#2B2623` text. Selected chips shift to a solid `#181615` fill with cream typography, or feature an inlaid bronze border when representing finishes (e.g., Oak, Travertine, Brass).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Strict square forms. Checked state displays an uncompromising solid charcoal fill with a crisp inner bronze checkmark or square pip. No rounded switches or bubbly toggles.

### Structural Dividers
- Hairline horizontal and vertical rules (`1px` solid `#E6E0D6`) used to delineate content sections, echoing layout grids found in architectural portfolios and technical blueprints.
